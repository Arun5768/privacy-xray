Installed

0xRay is watching the watchers.

From now on, every request a crypto app sends to another company is checked for your wallet address. Three steps and you're set.

  1. 01

    Pin 0xRay to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon, then the pin next to 0xRay. Its badge turns red the moment a page sends your address somewhere.

    🧩 3
  2. 02

    Watch it catch a leak

    Open the demo dApp, connect the throwaway demo wallet, then click the 0xRay icon. You'll see three leaks: plain, Base64 and a "hashed" one, with proof for each.

    Open the demo dApp →
  3. 03

    Use crypto like normal

    Connect your wallet anywhere as usual. Turn on Protect in the popup to stop requests carrying your address. RPC providers are never blocked, so dApps keep working.

Our promise

  • Nothing leaves your device. 0xRay makes no network requests of its own: no servers, no analytics, no account.
  • Never touches your keys. It only reads what pages send; it can't sign or move anything.
  • Open source. MIT licensed; read every line on GitHub.
