    For dApp teams

    Find the leak before your users do.

    Most teams don't know their analytics script collects wallet addresses. Under the EDPB's July 2026 blockchain guidelines, a wallet address linkable to a person is personal data, and hashing it doesn't change that.

    Open your own app

    Load the extension, connect a test wallet to your dApp, and click through your main flows: connect, swap, sign in.

    The popup lists every company that received the address, where it was hidden, how it was encoded and which script sent it.

    3

    Export the evidence

    Download a Markdown or JSON report with every finding, ready for your engineers or your data-protection lead.

    Evidence report

    Not a vibe. A receipt.

    Each finding names the request, the field, the encoding and the script responsible, so the fix is obvious: remove the identify call, stop sending the address, or hash nothing at all.
